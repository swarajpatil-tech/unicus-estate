01 — The idea

Land first. Brochure second.

Unicus Real Estate · Igatpuri, Nashik

Most weekend homes are bought from a brochure and forgiven later. We would rather you stood on the land first, in the hour before the light goes.

Unicus builds weekend homes in the hills above Igatpuri, where the Sahyadri range runs down to the Konkan and the monsoon arrives three weeks earlier than it does in the city.

The estate is a gated eighteen acres with 101 NA plots, sized from 2,500 to 5,000 sq ft. Titles are non-agricultural and clear, so you can build to your own plan instead of ours.

What you are buying is the view, the altitude, and a road that gets you there before Friday ends.

05 — Within the gate

Enough to stay a fortnight.

Community amenities, planned

Clubhouse

A shared hall and kitchen for the weekends when the whole family arrives at once.

Swimming pool

One pool, kept cool by the altitude, with a low deck for the evening.

Viewing decks

Two platforms set on the ridge for sunrise over the valley and the monsoon front.

Nature trails

Marked walks inside the estate boundary and out to the fort trail beyond it.

Gated security

Single entry, compound wall and a resident guard, so the house can stay empty between visits.

Power and water backup

Shared backup and a storage tank, because a hill house should not depend on the grid behaving.
